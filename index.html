Vessel Longevity

The Burning Ritual

Honoring a few mindful practices ensures your natural wax candle burns evenly, cleanly, and for its complete 55+ hour lifecycle.

01

The Memory Melt

On the very first burn, allow the candle to burn for 3 to 4 hours until the melted wax pool reaches all vessel edges. This prevents tunneling forever.

02

Trim the Cedarwood

Before each subsequent lighting, gently snap or pinch off the charred top of the cedarwood wick to 1/8–1/4 inch. This maintains an intimate, smoke-free flame.

03

Snuff, Don't Blow

Use a metal bell snuffer or simply place the vessel lid gently on top to extinguish the flame without scattering carbon particles or releasing acrid smoke.

04

The Vessel's Second Life

When 1/2 inch of wax remains, soak the vessel in warm hot water. Clean thoroughly to repurpose as an espresso cup, desktop brush pot, or succulent planter.
